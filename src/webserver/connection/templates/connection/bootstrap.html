Presentation

Which vocabulary the spectrogram panels and their dropdowns use. Applies to this screen only, and is remembered here — the other device keeps its own setting. Append ?labels=demo to the URL to open a display straight into the demo wording. demo also turns local enhancement off and hides its toggle, opens the controls for other participants, and makes model switches apply to everyone.

Remote control

Lets one device mute the others and switch their enhancement on or off, so a demo can be run from a single handset. Accepting is on by default — it is the same room-wide trust that already lets a peer change your model. Untick it to opt out; any command that is applied is announced on screen.
